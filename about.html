About

I'm Amir Foroughi — an independent mortgage agent with Real Mortgage Associates, working with buyers, owners, and business people across Toronto and Ontario, in English and in Farsi. Licensed by FSRA as a Mortgage Agent Level 2, which is what allows private and alternative lending — not every agent's licence covers it.

How this practice works

Independent, by structure.

I don't work for any lender — your file goes in front of forty-plus of them, and the offers compete. On standard residential files the winning lender pays the brokerage, so independence costs you nothing (OAC).

Penalty math is shown before a term is broken. Alternative lending is priced in dollars, not adjectives. And when staying with your current lender is the right answer, that's the recommendation you'll hear.

What you can hold me to

01

Numbers first — even the bad ones

Every recommendation arrives with its arithmetic attached. If a deal doesn't clear its costs, you'll hear 'wait' — and that answer is free.

02

Mortgages renew, mature, and change with your life. Your renewal gets flagged 120 days out and re-shopped, every term, without you asking.
